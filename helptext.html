Quick Start

  1. Place .txt files in your wildcards folder (one option per line).
  2. Use __filename__ in your prompt to pick a random line.
  3. Subfolders work too: __subfolder/filename__

Syntax

  • {red|green|blue} — pick one randomly
  • {2$$red|green|blue} — pick two
  • {1-3$$red|green|blue} — pick between 1 and 3
  • {0.5::red|0.3::green|0.2::blue} — weighted choices
  • __wildcardname__ — random line from wildcardname.txt
